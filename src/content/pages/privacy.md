## Public site

This website uses privacy-friendly, cookie-free basic analytics. It does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and protected areas are excluded from public analytics.

## Photographs

GPS coordinates and unnecessary EXIF metadata are removed before photographs are published. Public albums show only approximate locations.

## Protected content

Private albums and application materials require authentication. Necessary sessions expire automatically. Protected images and documents are not included in public search, RSS feeds, or sitemaps.

## Administrator access

The administrator email address is used only to deliver one-time sign-in verification codes.
